Our story

Rooted in the community. Since 1998.

For nearly three decades, Ludwig’s has been part of everyday life in upper Chester County. A quick lunch between errands, a dinner nobody had to cook, the holiday table, the wedding reception.

Our mission hasn’t changed: bring the tastiest, freshest and finest foods to the county, and serve them with a smile.
